Flour, Water, Salt & Time

Feeding Portland's soul with real heritage wild yeast bread.

At Crust & Crumb Sourdough, we believe that real bread cannot be rushed. Our bakery was born out of a simple, nostalgic pursuit: bringing the deep, complex flavors and blistered, deeply browned crusts of traditional mid-century European village bakers to our local neighborhood.

Every loaf we shape by hand begins with wild yeast, stone-ground Oregon-grown grain, and a lot of patience. Our starter, affectionately named Buster, has been fed daily for over a decade, imparting a signature bright, complex tang that commercial bakeries simply cannot replicate.

Our 36-Hour Sourdough Ritual

We bypass chemical yeasts, dough conditioners, and shortcuts. The deep flavor and digestible nature of our bread comes from meticulous fermentation.

01

1. Stone-Ground Heritage Flour

We partner with regional mills to source single-origin, organic heritage wheat. Stone-milling preserves the nutrient-dense germ and bran, producing a flour that retains its natural vitamins, oils, and deep, nutty flavor.

02

2. Long Cold Fermentation

Time does the heavy lifting. Our doughs undergo a full bulk fermentation, followed by an overnight rest in a cold room. This slow 36-hour proof allows wild bacteria to break down complex starches, making it easier on your stomach.

03

3. Baked on the Hearth

Our deck ovens feature heavy stone slabs. The wet, proofed boules hit the intense direct heat, generating a massive oven spring. Trapped steam cooks the interior crumb perfectly while building a heavy, mahogany-colored crust.
