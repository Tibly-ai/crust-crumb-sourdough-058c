How We Bake

The Hearth Process

We do not rush our dough. From stone miller to woven banneton baskets, we honor the ancient rhythm of cold-fermented breadmaking.

01

Mill & Mix

We source regional heirloom wheat grain and mill it stone-ground weekly to keep the rich wheatgerm oils alive and nutrient-dense.

02

Natural Rise

No commercial yeast is kept on-site. Our starter cultures, maintained daily for eight years, slowly build complex, bubbly lactic acid profiles.

03

Cold Cure

Preshaped loaves rest inside wicker banneton baskets in a cool room for up to 36 hours, breaking down starches into easy-to-digest enzymes.

04

Brick Steam

Baked directly on massive firebrick decks at high heat with rapid wet-steam injection for a glass-crisp, darkly caramelized exterior crust.
