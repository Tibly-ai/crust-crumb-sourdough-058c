The Bakery Way

We believe the best things in life take thirty-six hours to rise.

At Crust & Crumb Sourdough, we honor traditional European fermentation. No industrial shortcuts, no artificial additives, and absolutely no rush. Just organic flour, filtered mountain water, coastal sea salt, and a thriving wild culture passed down for generations.
